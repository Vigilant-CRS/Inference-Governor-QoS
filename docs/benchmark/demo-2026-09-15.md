# Demo: four cameras, one GPU — and the runs where the governor did not help

Recorded 15 September 2026 on an RTX 3070 Laptop GPU (8 GB, driver 580.178.04),
Triton 2.70. Tools: `vig-bench` binary `demo-record` (timelines) and
`tools/demo/render.py` (video). Configurations: [`examples/demo/`](../../examples/demo/).

## What was measured

A 40-second city-driving clip is played as a camera at 30 frames per second.
Detectors (RF-DETR, public COCO weights, Apache-2.0) and a vision-language model
(SmolVLM-500M-Instruct, Apache-2.0, "Name the main things on the road ahead in a
few words", at most 5 tokens) get its frames — once **directly** from NVIDIA
Triton, once **through the governor** in the same process. The two arms run one
after the other on the same GPU with the same frames; running them at the same
time would make them slow each other down.

Two Triton processes serve the models: RF-DETR (ONNX Runtime) and SmolVLM
(Python backend; `examples/demo/triton-vlm/`). Every request and its answer,
refusal or error is a line in a timeline. The video shows, for each displayed
frame, only results that had **arrived** by then; boxes are drawn where the
detector saw the objects, so late results visibly lag.

Runtime profiles: RF-DETR Medium p50/p95/p99 19.0/20.7/21.8 ms and Small
15.0/16.9/19.0 ms (`vig profile`, 200 runs); SmolVLM 121/156/170 ms
(`demo-record` in `profile-vlm` mode, 100 runs over gRPC).

"Fresh" means: sampled every contract period, the latest result that had
arrived was at most `max_age_ms` old.

## Results

| Run | Load | Front detector fresh, direct → governor | Front detector age at arrival p50 | Language model answers in 40 s | Config |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Four cameras, city driving** | 4 × RF-DETR Medium at 30 fps (≈ 230 % of the GPU) + SmolVLM | **0.2 % → 100 %** | 336 ms → 53 ms | 223 → 2 | `krakow-cams4.yaml` |
| **Four cameras, sidewalk robot** | same load, front camera useless after 150 ms (walking pace) | **0.2 % → 100 %** | 337 ms → 62 ms | 197 → 2 | `sidewalk-cams4.yaml` |
| **Four cameras, humanoid robot** | same load: head, chest and two wrist cameras, head camera useless after 100 ms | **0.1 % → 99.8 %** | 334 ms → 53 ms | 165 → 3 | `humanoid-cams4.yaml` |
| One camera, 10 Hz | RF-DETR Small + SmolVLM | 100 % → 99.7 % | 20 ms → 63 ms | 323 → 284 | `krakow-vig.yaml` |
| One camera, 30 fps, serialised | RF-DETR Medium + SmolVLM, `slots: 1`, `no_corun` | 100 % → 99.7 % | 42 ms → 39 ms | 277 → 2 | `krakow-sat-slots1.yaml` |
| One camera, 30 fps, two slots | same, `slots: 2`, no `no_corun` | 100 % → 100 % | 42 ms → 45 ms | 277 → 260 | `krakow-sat-slots2.yaml` |

In the four-camera runs the three other cameras (class `normal`) went from
0.1–0.3 % fresh on the direct path to about 32 % (city, 100 ms freshness),
66 % (sidewalk, 150 ms) and 40 % (humanoid, 100 ms) under the governor: it
served them with what the protected camera left and discarded frames a newer
one had replaced (about 720–830 per camera).

## What this says

- **Where the GPU is overloaded, the governor decides what gives.** With four
  cameras, Triton computes every frame in arrival order, and every result is a
  third of a second old when it arrives — for all cameras, including the one
  that matters. Under the governor the protected front camera stays fresh in
  every cycle; the other cameras get the rest; the language model waits.
- **The price is shown, not hidden.** The language model answered twice in 40
  seconds under the governor. That is the contract as written (`best_effort`);
  a deployment that needs its answers must give it a budget or other hardware.
- **Where the GPU is not overloaded, the governor does not help — and can
  hurt.** One camera plus the language model runs fine directly: two Triton
  processes share this GPU without the detector ever getting late. Serialising
  them (`slots: 1`, `no_corun`, a hand-written configuration) made the detector
  older and starved the language model. With two slots the governor was as good
  as the direct path. This is the case `vig autotune` exists for: measure
  concurrency and interference instead of assuming them.

## Limits

- One GPU, one clip, one run per configuration; no repetitions.
- The four cameras show the same street, offset in time; only the front camera
  is displayed. Detection quality was not evaluated.
- The direct client keeps at most four open requests per camera and drops newer
  frames beyond that (736 per camera in the four-camera run), exactly like the
  load driver of `vig-fit`.
- SmolVLM's answers are short by design (5 tokens); longer answers make one call
  a longer non-interruptible block (p99 858 ms at 16 tokens).
